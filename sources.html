Sources & receipts

Every monitored page, its fetch result, and the SHA-256 receipt of the exact bytes captured. Raw HTML captures are published under data/raw/ so every quote on this site can be re-checked against its source.

Captures

Reading a receipt

  • captured — the source answered at build time and its bytes were saved and hashed.
  • stale capture — the source failed at build time; the newest available capture is shown and the failure is recorded in the dataset.
  • unreachable — no capture exists; the source is a documented gap (transport errors recorded).
  • SHA-256 — identifies the exact fetch. Re-downloading a page can produce different bytes (per-request markup); compare against the published capture file, not a fresh download.
